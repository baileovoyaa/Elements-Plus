## Circuit Experiment

Here you can test your own circuits and gradually unlock various advanced components.

Insert your circuit diagram into the slot above and click the "Start Experiment" button, and the experiment will be automatically performed.

After the experiment is successful, you will obtain a compiled circuit diagram, which is associated with a component. It can be used directly as a component in the lithography machine, or used in the "Manufacture" tab of the lithography machine to manufacture chips.

Note: Compiled circuit diagrams cannot be edited further, but you can create a copy of the circuit diagram in the lithography machine manufacturing page, and uncheck the "Keep Compiled" option. The copy will no longer have the compiled status and can be edited further.

If the experiment fails, the progress bar will turn red, and hovering over it will show the specific failure reason. The state of the input components in the circuit diagram will be frozen at the failed test case, and you can view the specific failure logic by placing it in the lithography machine later.
